# SDLC Phase 1: Planning

## When to use
- Starting a new development cycle.
- Kicking off a major feature.
- Re-planning after scope change.

## Deliverables
1. `PROJECT_CHARTER.md` — vision, goals, success metrics.
2. `SCOPE.md` — in-scope, out-of-scope, assumptions.
3. `RISKS.md` — risks with likelihood × impact.
4. `TIMELINE.md` — milestones and estimated dates.
5. `STAKEHOLDERS.md` — who cares, communication plan.

## Activities
- Elicit requirements from stakeholders.
- Define measurable success criteria.
- Estimate effort (T-shirt sizing: S/M/L/XL).
- Identify dependencies.
- Identify risks and mitigations.
- Get human approval on charter.

## Exit Criteria
- [ ] Charter approved by human.
- [ ] Scope frozen for the cycle.
- [ ] Risks documented with mitigations.
- [ ] Success metrics are measurable.
- [ ] Stakeholders identified.

## Anti-Patterns
- ❌ Skipping planning ("we know what to do")
- ❌ Vague success criteria
- ❌ Unbounded scope
- ❌ Ignoring risks
