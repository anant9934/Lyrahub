# Operational Cost Management

## When to use
- Monthly ops review.
- Planning monitoring stack.
- Forecasting support load.

## Cost Categories
| Category | Free Tier | Paid Trigger |
|----------|-----------|--------------|
| Monitoring (BetterStack) | 10 monitors | >10 → $29/mo |
| Error tracking (GlitchTip) | Self-host free | — |
| Email (Resend) | 3K/month | >3K → $20/mo |
| Backups (R2) | 10 GB | >10 GB → $0.015/GB |
| Support hours | ₹0 (self) | If hired |

## Support Load Estimate
- 100 users: 2 hrs/week
- 500 users: 8 hrs/week
- 1000 users: 20 hrs/week
- 5000 users: 40 hrs/week (part-time hire)

## Anti-Patterns
- ❌ No monitoring
- ❌ No backups
- ❌ Underestimating support
