# /compliance-check — Regulatory Compliance Audit

## Steps

1. **@security-compliance** leads.
2. Verify GDPR: consent, access, erasure, portability.
3. Verify DPDP: consent, localization, grievance officer.
4. Verify WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast, keyboard, screen reader.
5. Verify NAAC/NBA data readiness.
6. Verify third-party register.
7. Update `COMPLIANCE_MATRIX.md`.

## Output

- Compliance matrix updated.
- Gaps identified with remediation.
- Audit-ready documentation.
