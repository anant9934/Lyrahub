# /threat-model — Threat Modeling Session

## Steps

1. Invoke **@security-threat-model** to lead the session.
2. Define scope: feature/architecture under review.
3. Draw data flow diagram.
4. Identify trust boundaries.
5. Apply STRIDE per component.
6. Build attack trees for critical flows.
7. Assess risk (likelihood × impact).
8. Recommend mitigations.
9. Update `THREAT_MODEL.md`.

## Output

- Updated `THREAT_MODEL.md` with new threats and mitigations.
- Risk register updated.
- Prioritized mitigation list.
