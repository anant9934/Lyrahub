# Department Leadership: Head of Department

## Overview
The Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning is led by **Dr. S. Radhakrishnan**, Senior Professor and Head of Department. 

## Contact & Office Details
- **Office Location**: Department Block 3, Room 301 (Third Floor)
- **Email**: hod@aiml.hub
- **Office Hours**: Monday & Wednesday, 2:30 PM – 4:30 PM (Students & Parents); Tuesday & Thursday 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM (Faculty Consultations)
- **Appointment Protocol**: Prior scheduling via the department office or Lyrahub dashboard is recommended.

## Department Vision & Priorities
1. **Curriculum Modernization**: Integration of modern Large Language Models, Agentic Architectures, and Edge AI into the undergraduate syllabus.
2. **Industry Alignment**: Strong industry partnerships for live capstones, dual-degree tracks, and 100% placement readiness.
3. **Research Excellence**: Fostering undergraduate publications in tier-1 venues (NeurIPS, CVPR, ACL, IEEE Trans).
