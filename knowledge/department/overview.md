# AI & ML Department — Lyrahub

## Overview

The AI & ML Department is a specialized academic department offering programs in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at the undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels.

Lyrahub is the department's integrated enterprise portal — managing students, faculty, projects, events, rankings, alumni, and AI-powered departmental intelligence.

## Structure

- **Head of Department (HOD)**: Leads academic and administrative operations
- **Coordinator of Studies (COS)**: Manages curriculum and course scheduling
- **Head of Section (HOS)**: Oversees student sections
- **Faculty**: Teaching and research staff
- **Students**: B.Tech, M.Tech, and PhD scholars

## Programs Offered

- B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (4 years)
- M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence (2 years)
- PhD in AI/ML (3–5 years)

## Key Metrics

- Department ranking system: AHP + TOPSIS multi-criteria model
- QR-based attendance tracking system
- AI-powered AIDA assistant for departmental intelligence
- Alumni network management

## Research Focus Areas

- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Computer Vision
- Generative AI
- Reinforcement Learning
- MLOps and AI Systems
- Responsible AI

## Contact

For all queries, use the Lyrahub portal or contact the department office.
