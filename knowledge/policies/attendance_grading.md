# Department Academic Policies

## 1. Attendance Policy
- **Minimum Threshold**: Students are required to maintain a minimum of **75% attendance** in each individual course to be eligible to sit for semester-end examinations.
- **Medical Condonation**: Attendance between 65% and 74% may be condoned by the Head of Department upon formal submission of authorized medical certificates within 3 days of returning to campus.
- **Below 65%**: Students falling below 65% attendance are strictly detained from appearing in the final examinations for that course and must repeat the course during summer terms.

## 2. Grading Scale (10-Point System)
- **O (Outstanding)**: 90–100% marks (Grade Point: 10.0)
- **A+ (Excellent)**: 80–89% marks (Grade Point: 9.0)
- **A (Very Good)**: 70–79% marks (Grade Point: 8.0)
- **B+ (Good)**: 60–69% marks (Grade Point: 7.0)
- **B (Above Average)**: 55–59% marks (Grade Point: 6.0)
- **C (Pass)**: 50–54% marks (Grade Point: 5.0)
- **F (Fail)**: Below 50% marks (Grade Point: 0.0)
