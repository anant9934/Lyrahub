# Campus Placement Eligibility Criteria

## General Eligibility Rules
1. **Academic Standing**: Minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of **7.00** across all completed semesters without active backlogs at the time of drive registration.
2. **Backlog Rules**:
   - Zero active/standing backlogs are permitted during placement recruitment seasons.
   - Cleared past backlogs are subject to individual corporate partner criteria.
3. **Department Attendance**: Minimum 75% attendance record during the current academic year.

## Company Tier Classifications
- **Tier 1 (Dream / Marquee)**: CTC ≥ ₹18.0 LPA. Open to all students with CGPA ≥ 8.00 and top 20% departmental rank.
- **Tier 2 (Super Dream)**: CTC between ₹10.0 LPA and ₹17.99 LPA. Open to students with CGPA ≥ 7.50.
- **Tier 3 (Core & IT)**: CTC up to ₹9.99 LPA. Open to all eligible students with CGPA ≥ 6.50.
